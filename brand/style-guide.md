The look in one line: every place is **cut open and seen from outside**, with warm off-white cut faces, ink cut edges and hatch; inside it, **the objects are as real as we can build them and the people are stylized** (a recurring cast with dot eyes); the hidden system is the only orange, and it runs through the walls and the ground, out of the room and back.

**Motion principles** (owner feedback 2026-10-15, "everything feels static except the camera"; rough-cut review, "we still see both mannequins, it's kind of boring; animation is also what people want to see").
0. **The motion rule** (✅ owner decision, rough-cut review). **Something meaningful moves on screen at least every 2 to 3 s; never two people sitting still under narration. Every beat that explains a mechanism gets a visual of that mechanism** (the wake word: the phone cut open and its chip listening, § 3; a study: The Lab running it, § 3). *Meaningful* (🧪, so it can be checked): a change a viewer notices that belongs to the line: a gesture (type, lift a phone, glance, lean, point), a glide (§ 8), the route drawing or dots travelling, a mechanism animating (a chip's waveform, a robot arm tapping, the counter ticking, the meter's needle), a detail box or card entering. Breathing, blinks, the lamp and the idle drift keep a shot alive but **do not count**. Checked in Studio playback, not on stills.
1. **Something is always alive.** Figures breathe (±0.012 units, phase-offset per person), blink (eye scale y → 0.1 and back over 4 frames, every 3 to 5 s, seeded per character, the first within 3 s), nod gently, shift weight, thumbs scroll, glance (head turns up to 0.5 rad toward what the line points at), lean in to a screen, lift a phone when it lights.
2. **The set:** Night: screen light flickers on faces, the lamp breathes ±3%. Day: leaves pass the window, dust in the sun. Orange ambient life (LED pulse) only while the system is active.
3. **Calm and slow;** the orange is the only fast thing, except the "feel it" shake while the system is physically happening (§ 8 table).
4. **The motion check** (✅ owner decision, 2026-10-27; values 🧪; checked by the visual-reviewer on 3 consecutive frames per moving scene, `.claude/agents/visual-reviewer.md`). Stills hide what the owner catches on the phone; a failure is a fix, never a note.
   - **Wheels roll forward.** Spokes advance in the direction of travel, never more than half a spoke gap a frame (as built, `wheels: 'forward'`, the default: ≤ 26°/frame on a 5-spoke rim, 13° on a 10-blade one, the spokes smeared above it while the road keeps the real speed). `wheels: 'legacy'` only to re-render a posted video unchanged.
   - **Props move with the world.** Signs, markers, poles, the ruler and every prop stream past at the car's speed while it moves; nothing stays fixed beside a moving car, nothing moves once it has stopped. A parked car's world doesn't scroll.
   - **The same object across cuts.** Same body, shape, paint, lamp state (brake, hazards; hazard phase on the video clock), charge or fill state, road and time of day on both sides of every slide, glide or cut.
   - **Stopped cars show lit brake or hazard lamps.** **Open (owner to confirm):** this came from a car stopping on a road (No. 009); No. 010's cars are **parked in a charging bay with lamps off**. Recommended scope: cars that stop in traffic or break down show brake or hazard lamps; cars parked (engine off, in a bay or a driveway) show none.

## Review checklist (one still per scene, `yarn stills <slug>`)

1. **Outside, always:** apart from screen bookends (if used) and full-frame screens, every shot looks into a slice from outside at the video's one fixed angle; cut faces visible in `cut` off-white with the fine 45° hatch, an ink line on every cut edge and on nothing else; no interior, eye-level or over-the-shoulder shot. Wide shots: sky band on top (to ≈ y 430), room in the middle, cut ground in the bottom ≈ 20%.
2. **Orange:** the only pixels in the reserved band (hue 8 to 40°, sat ≥ 60%, lightness 35 to 75%) are the system (path, nodes, dots, its light, active LEDs, its mark on a screen: the ad's edge or a `'system'` mic indicator, the viewer's light channel). Other channel colours (§ 1 Light channels) only inside a fibre or optical component, no spill, no bloom; the viewer's channel is the orange one; the video says once that the colours are added. Captions, tags, callouts, cards, people and cut faces are never orange; nor are non-system parts of realistic objects (flex cables black, no amber parts, labels or LEDs). Dashboard warning icons and indicator lights are `warning` yellow `#eedd2b`, flat, no bloom or halo. Real red indicators on objects (a meter's leak triangle) are `leakRed` `#ef3957`, flat, no emissive, bloom or halo. Go/on/flowing lights (signal green, cluster arrows, a charge-port light) are `signalGreen` `#1fcf9f`, steady, flat; brake and rear hazard lamps `brakeLit`, front hazards and light bars `warning`, none bloomed. Inside a card only the system's mark is orange (an ad's edge, a chart's data line, the dispatch circle).
3. **Phones face their owners, devices generic:** no screen turned to the camera in the slice; every readable screen is in a detail box, one of ≤ 2 full-frame screens, or a bookend (not counted); phone UI generic (no real app, logo, brand colour, "9:41"); no logo, wordmark or maker's signature shape on any device (phone camera = a single plain strip).
4. **Mood:** one mood per slice (or the single time-passes beat, rendered once with light and colour blending, nothing double-exposed); Night: subject p50 ≥ 30%, cut faces read off-white; Day: brightest near-neutral ≤ 96%, zero pixels at 255,255,255 (overlays, screens, glow and small glass/metal glints masked; no pure-white surface).
5. **Frame:** title block only in the first scene (top y 300; from No. 002 the question is up at frame 0 and holds to ≈ 0.8 s after it is spoken; on a black wake-bookend opening, No. 001, the old block enters at ≈ 2.8 s); one-line statements in the sky band (top y 288), two-line statements low (bottom y 1000); no source text anywhere on screen; nothing readable past x 888 or below y 1248; left-aligned elements start at x 120; the subject (≈ y 820 to 1180 on wide shots) not covered by a 2-line caption page (§ 7 known overlap).
6. **Captions:** margin note (square, left rule, `capBg`), Inter 700 50 px, words appear as spoken, no highlight or underline, bottom y 1188, ≤ 5 words; off while a statement is up.
7. **Tags:** colour square + role, elbow leader, in the margin outside the slice; ≤ 2; never with a card; first appearance only (and on re-entry). Car tags: leader from the roof, the square in the paint with a 2 px ink outline, the name as spoken, in the sky above the lane.
8. **One idea per frame:** at most one card (detail box, full-frame screen, callout, counter, statement, chart card, place label) besides title block and captions; nothing riding along with it. Callouts are labels with an elbow leader and a paper halo, no card.
9. **Statement cards:** ≤ 2, Archivo 88 px left-aligned, at the § 7 positions. No attribution line, "Source:" or URL on screen (sources are in the post description).
10. **System:** route ends hidden inside the bodies, coming out behind the shoulders in a low arc (never crossing a body); leaves through the wall and ground on the cut face; node dots at devices; X-ray at most once, as a cut-away (hatched reveal edges, ink line, dark cavity) opening top-down; trace present on every later shot of that slice; next slice opens clean.
11. **Cast:** dot eyes, no mouth; adult proportions; sleeves to the wrist, small hands; colours as § 8; something alive in every shot (breath, blink, glance).
12. **Opening and loop** (from No. 002): frame 0 is the surprise image (not black, no fade-in, not the X-ray) with the question in the title block; the voice starts by ≈ 0.3 s, its first sentence carries the surprise, the question is spoken by ≈ 5 to 6 s; the last shot ends on frame 0's picture (same slice or screen, mood, framing, things lit). **Screen-bookend loop** (No. 001, or a new video where the owner chose the black pair): frame 0 black, the screen wakes (8 frames after ≈ 0.2 s dark; lock screen lit by ≈ 0.5 s, first app action, the story's time, never 9:41), the screen shrinks onto its phone as the camera pulls back and hands over to the 3D phone, wide by ≈ 3 s; at the end a push into a phone's screen, it locks (black in 2 frames, soft click), 4 black frames; last frame black. No title card, logo or overlay text over the wake: the title block enters at ≈ 2.8 s (§ 6). On every full-frame bookend screen (lock, search, any app; not the § 6 full-frame screen box): nothing readable above y 520 (lock clock ≈ y 640), no text past x 888, long rows truncated (§ 8 screen placement).
13. **Transitions and camera:** same slice and mood `continue` (no dissolve); a framing change in one location is a 0.5 to 1.0 s cubic glide, never a cut; new slice (incl. The Lab) `slide` (18 frames, from below) or `follow` (24 frames, seam line visible); a time-passes beat is a 1.5 s light-and-colour blend of one render; hard cuts rare (≤ 2 outside the bookend lock, 🧪); no 12-frame dissolve between Section scenes unless chosen on purpose.
14. **Motion (Studio playback, not stills):** in every 2 to 3 s window something meaningful moves (§ 8 motion rule; breathing, blinks and drift don't count); never two people sitting still under narration; every mechanism beat shows that mechanism (object cut-away or The Lab, § 3), cut faces hatched and inked, non-system glows not orange. A "feel it" shake (§ 8) runs only while its cause is on screen, kicks on the onset, never in slow motion, with the music dipped and the captions still. **Motion check** (§ 8 principle 4, 3 consecutive frames): wheels roll forward (≤ half a spoke gap a frame), props move with the world and stop with it, the same car, paint, lamps and time of day across every cut, stopped cars' lamps as § 8 says.
15. **Objects real, people stylized:** objects read as real (physical materials, HDRI reflections, contact shadows / AO, real detail where the eye goes; a teardown reads like a repair photo), the cast stays rounded with dot eyes, the cut faces keep their ink edge and hatch; every third-party asset on screen is in `public/assets/LICENSES.md`.
