## Description (YouTube)

Why is a BYD so much cheaper than a Tesla?

A BYD the size of a Tesla Model 3 starts at about half the price in China. Analysts took one apart to find out why.

BYD in the US: let it in or keep it out?

In China, the cheapest BYD Seal 06 EV, a sedan about the size of a Model 3, lists at 109,900 yuan, against 235,500 yuan for a rear-wheel-drive Tesla Model 3. People often credit subsidies and cheap labor, but Tesla builds the Model 3 in China too. Analysts who took a BYD Seal apart in 2023 found about three quarters of its parts were made by BYD, battery included, and estimated it cost about 15 percent less to build than a China-made Model 3.

Dollar figures are converted from China list prices (¥109,900 and ¥235,500) at about 6.7 yuan per dollar; exchange rates move. They are not US prices.

Tesla's annual report says it buys its battery cells from suppliers like Panasonic and CATL. Engineers who took apart BYD's smaller Seagull found the battery, motors, power electronics, body panels and lights were all made in-house. The price gap is wider than that build-cost estimate. Part of it is range, since the cheapest Seal 06 trim goes less far than the Model 3, and part is BYD's price cuts in China's price war. No source says how much each one explains.

In the US, BYD doesn't sell its passenger cars. There's a 100 percent tariff on Chinese-made EVs, and starting with 2027 models, a federal rule bans connected-car software linked to China.

Illustrative: the teardown room is staged, and so are the parts grid and the mechanic, who is made up. The two cars are shown side by side in one room for the story; in reality they don't sit together. Three of four parts lit is the analysts' 75 percent figure drawn as a picture, not the real parts list. The price tags in the opening show China list prices converted to dollars, not US prices. The bars are shapes, each row on its own scale: the build-cost bar is an analyst teardown estimate (2023) of the BYD Seal vs a Shanghai-built Model 3; the price bar is 2026 China list prices, the cheapest BYD Seal 06 EV vs the Tesla Model 3 rear-wheel drive. They are different BYD models. The Tesla and the BYD are drawn to resemble the real models, with no logos or badges; their interiors are simplified and not the real layouts. The smaller BYD and the other cars are generic. The supplier crates and the blade cells are generic, and the pack emptying and refilling is a re-enactment. The faded car in the US scene is generic too.
