Your phone is not listening to you. Probably not. Researchers went looking for apps that secretly record you. They found none sending audio. They did find analytics code inside some apps recording the screen. So how did it know about the coffee machine? You never searched it. Your friend did. Same Wi-Fi. Same evening. Same couch. Every device on your Wi-Fi often shares the same public address. To an ad company, that can look like one household. Your phone. Your laptop. Your friend's phone. One home. Your friend's search can become your ad. Companies mix what you log into with what they guess. Now zoom out. One data company says it has 126 million US households on its map. It feels like mind reading. It isn't. It doesn't need your voice. Sharing a Wi-Fi can be enough.
