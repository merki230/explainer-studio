## Title

Why would a tire need its own radio?

Use it as the YouTube Shorts title (36 characters). TikTok and Instagram have no title field, so it isn't used there.

## Description (YouTube)

In many cars, a little radio inside each tire wakes up as you drive away.

Illustrative: the sensor IDs shown (like 4F2A) and the rows of tires in the lab are an example, not real data.

Each sensor has its own battery and ID number, and it sends the car a reading about once a minute. Some cars skip the sensors and spot a soft tire because it spins slightly faster.

Sources: US federal tire pressure monitoring rule (49 CFR 571.138), NHTSA's 2012 report on how well the warning works, Tire Review (2015), Continental Tires.
