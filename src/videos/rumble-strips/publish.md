## Description (YouTube)

How do rumble strips work? Why does the road make a loud noise when you drift onto the highway shoulder?

Rumble strips are grooves cut into the pavement to warn drowsy or distracted drivers. The same grooves can be spaced to make a musical road, like the one in Lancaster, California.

Illustrative: the mile number and the guide-sign text, the compressed sign spacing, the slow motion, the groove depth (drawn about 2.5 times deeper than real), the drawn vibration path, and the musical road's groove layout, tune and house are examples, not real data.
