# Video ideas

Ideas waiting for a slot. A new video starts by agreeing the angle with the owner (see the playbook), then research.

## Testing: hidden technology inside ordinary objects (owner, 2026-10-18)

No. 002 (the radio inside the tire) drew strong comments, views and likes. The working recipe:

1. An object everyone owns but never thinks about.
2. A hidden piece of technology inside it that nobody suspects.
3. One surprise that fits in a sentence.
4. A real-life payoff the viewer can check ("next time you fill a tire…").

This is a hypothesis, not a rule: judge it on two or three more videos (metrics in `brand/feedback.md`).

| # | Object | The hidden surprise | Sample hook | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water or gas meter | Many meters have a small radio; a utility vehicle reads them by driving past | "A truck can read your water meter just by driving past your house." | **Next: No. 004** |
| 2 | Shopping cart | A wire buried around the parking lot signals the wheel to lock if the cart leaves | "There's a wire buried around the supermarket parking lot, and it's talking to the cart's wheels." | idea |
| 3 | Car key | Each press sends a new code that's never reused (rolling code) | "Your car key changes its password every time you press it." | idea |
| 4 | Pet microchip | A grain-sized chip with no battery, powered for an instant by the scanner | "The chip in your dog has no battery, and it still answers when someone asks." | idea |
| 5 | Hotel key card | A chip and antenna inside the plastic; the door checks a code | "Your hotel key card has a tiny antenna hidden inside the plastic." | idea |
| 6 | Car airbag | A sensor decides in milliseconds whether a crash is real | "Your airbag decides you're crashing faster than you can blink." | idea |
| 7 | Parking garage lights | A sensor over each space reports whether it's taken | "That green light above the parking space is watching whether a car is under it." | idea |
| 8 | Tire sensors (follow-up to No. 002) | After a tire rotation, how does the car know which sensor is on which wheel? (relearn, or automatic location) | "How does your car know which tire is which?" | idea (from a No. 002 TikTok comment) |

All claims above are unverified until researched.
